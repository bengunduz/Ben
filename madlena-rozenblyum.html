About Madlena

Madlena Rozenblyum, LCSW, is a seasoned Licensed Clinical Social Worker and psychotherapist practicing in Forest Hills, Queens. A native of Georgia during the Soviet era, she immigrated to the United States in 1989 — an experience that gives her a deep, personal understanding of the challenges of emigration, adjustment and building a new life.

A native Russian speaker who is fluent in English, she offers a warm, culturally attuned space for individuals and families from Russian-speaking communities as well as English-speaking clients.

Healing begins in a space where you are truly understood — in your language, and in your story.

Areas of focus

Individual, child and family therapy addressing:

Anxiety & Panic

Tools to manage worry, panic attacks and overwhelming stress.

Trauma

Complex trauma, war trauma and the after-effects of domestic violence.

Emigration & Adjustment

Support through relocation, cultural transition and identity.

Depression & Grief

Working through loss, low mood and life transitions.

Family & Relationships

Parent-child and spousal conflicts, communication and repair.

Sleep Disorders

Addressing insomnia and sleep difficulties linked to stress.

Humanitarian work

Beyond her private practice, Madlena is recognized for her active role in humanitarian mental health support. She has been prominently involved in organizing grassroots and professional networks providing psychological support to refugees and people affected by conflict — particularly during the crises in Crimea and mainland Ukraine.

Author & educator

Madlena is also an author and educator who writes articles and books offering practical, compassionate parenting advice — helping parents understand their children and strengthen family bonds.
